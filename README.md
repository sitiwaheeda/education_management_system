# Bimbel Management System

Web-based management system for managing bimbel operations across multiple branches.

## Features

- Student Management
- Parent Management
- Teacher Management
- Program & Class Management
- Schedule Management
- Student & Teacher Attendance
- Enrollment Management
- Billing & Payment
- Digital Receipt
- Reporting
